# Mission

`MISSION.md` is the compact reason for learning. Every lesson and next action should trace back to it.

```markdown
# Mission: Topic

## Why

One to three sentences describing the concrete outcome.

## Success looks like

- An observable ability.

## Constraints

- Time, source, or learning preferences.

## Out of scope

- Adjacent work deliberately deferred.
```

Keep one mission per workspace and keep it shorter than one screen. Prefer observable outcomes to "understand X". Missions may evolve; confirm the change, update the file, and create a learning record explaining why it changes future teaching.
